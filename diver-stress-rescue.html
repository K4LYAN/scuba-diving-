SSI Diver Stress & Rescue

Learn to spot stress early, prevent problems, and help another diver when something goes wrong.

Send an enquiry
Continuing educationStep 04 of 5

About the course

Stress is behind most diving incidents. This programme teaches you to recognise it in yourself and your buddy, prevent problems before they escalate, and manage a rescue if one is needed.

Who it’s for

Certified divers who want to recognise and manage problems underwater.

02 — What You’ll Learn

Skills this course gives you

  • Recognising stress in yourself and others
  • Preventing problems before they grow
  • Rescue skills and techniques
  • Diving with greater awareness

03 — Course Pathway

Where this course fits

SSI training builds from your first certification to professional level.
