01 — About Us

About Dive Adda

An internationally certified dive centre, proudly affiliated with SSI, born out of a life spent working under water.

Our full story

Dive Adda was founded by a professional with 15 years of service in the Indian Navy, over a decade in submarines and 10 years in the oil & gas industry. That experience under water is what the centre is built on.

Today our SSI certified instructors take complete beginners on their first breath underwater, train divers up to professional level, and run snorkeling and water activities in Visakhapatnam and Rajahmundry.

03 — Scuba Diving

Scuba DivingVizag Visakhapatnam, Andhra Pradesh

Breathe underwater for the first time off the coast of Vizag.

Your morning starts with a classroom briefing at our SSI certified centre: how your equipment works, the signals you will use and the plan for your dive. Then it is a short 10–15 minute boat ride out to the dive site, where an experienced instructor takes you down and stays beside you the whole way.

No experience is needed to start. Already certified? Guided dives and SSI courses take you further.

  • ≈ 40 minunderwater per person
  • 6–8 mmaximum depth for first-timers
  • Age 8+no experience needed
  • Photos & videoof your dive, included

04 — Water Sports

Water SportsRajahmundry On the Godavari, Andhra Pradesh

Swap the sea for the Godavari.

At Rajahmundry the Godavari runs wide beneath its landmark bridges, and it is where the Dive Adda team runs an afternoon’s worth of river adventure: speed boat rides, jet skis and kayaks for anyone who wants the thrill of the water without going under it.

Bringing friends or family? The dragon, disco and bumper rides are towed rides made for groups and plenty of laughs. Every ride is run by our team, and timings are confirmed when you book.
